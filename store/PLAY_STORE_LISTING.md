## Full description  (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OSolitaire is a clean, elegant take on the timeless card game of Klondike
Solitaire — the classic you already know, refined for a calm, distraction-free
play session on your phone.

No ads. No sign-up. No internet required. Just you and the cards.

• CLASSIC KLONDIKE
Play the solitaire you grew up with. Build the four foundations from Ace to King
and clear the tableau to win.

• ONE-TAP OR DRAW-THREE
Choose how you deal from the stock: draw one card at a time for a relaxed game,
or draw three for a greater challenge. Switch any time with a clear, labelled
control.

• SMART, TAP-TO-MOVE CONTROLS
Tap a card and it travels to the best available spot — foundation first, then
the tableau. No fiddly dragging required, though every move stays fully in your
control. Invalid moves give a gentle shake so you always know where you stand.

• UNLIMITED UNDO
Changed your mind? Step back through your moves with unlimited undo and explore
a different line of play.

• PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Your games are saved automatically on your device. Keep several deals on the go
and resume any of them exactly where you stopped — even after closing the app.

• A LAYOUT THAT FITS YOUR HAND
Prefer the deck on the left or the right? Tap the title to cycle through tidy
top-row arrangements and put the pile you use most within easy thumb reach.

• DESIGNED TO BE READABLE
Large, crisp card faces that stay perfectly legible regardless of your phone's
system font size — so the game always looks the way it should.

• TRULY OFFLINE & PRIVATE
OSolitaire works entirely on your device. It collects no personal data, shows no
advertising, and requires no account. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing
tracking you.

• SUPPORT THE DEVELOPER (OPTIONAL)
OSolitaire is free with no strings attached. If you enjoy it, there is an
optional "buy me a coffee" link — purely a thank-you, with no rewards, no
unlocked features, and no pressure. The game is complete and free for everyone.

Whether you have five minutes in a queue or a quiet evening to unwind,
OSolitaire is ready — elegant, fast, and always ad-free.

Shuffle up and deal.
(about 2,050 characters — well within the 4000 limit)
