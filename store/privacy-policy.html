Privacy Policy for OSolitaire

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the OSolitaire mobile application ("the app") handles information. The app is provided free of charge by the developer ("we", "us").

Summary

OSolitaire does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device and requires no internet connection to play.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal information. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not use advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and preferences (such as saved deals, the draw mode, sound setting, and layout choice) in your device's local application storage. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the data from within the app or uninstall the app.

Network access

The app itself makes no network requests. It contains one optional link — "buy me a coffee" — which, when tapped, opens the developer's donation page in your device's own web browser. That page is operated by a third party (Ko-fi). If you choose to follow that link, any information you provide there is handled under that third party's privacy policy, not ours. Following the link is entirely optional and provides no rewards or features in the app.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
